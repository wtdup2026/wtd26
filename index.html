About the day

Why World Toilet Day matters

World Toilet Day is observed every year on 19 November to inspire action on the global sanitation crisis and to accelerate progress towards Sustainable Development Goal 6 — water and sanitation for all by 2030. It began as an initiative of the World Toilet Organization in 2001 and has been an official United Nations observance since 2013.

The 2026 campaign, “Safe toilets. Equal futures.”, focuses on the link between sanitation and gender equality — calling for toilets that are safe, private and accessible for women, girls and everyone else, without discrimination.
