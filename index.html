About the day

Why World Toilet Day matters

World Toilet Day is observed every year on 19 November to inspire action on the global sanitation crisis and to accelerate progress towards Sustainable Development Goal 6 — water and sanitation for all by 2030. It began as an initiative of the World Toilet Organization in 2001 and has been an official United Nations observance since 2013.

The 2026 campaign, “Safe toilets. Equal futures.”, focuses on the link between sanitation and gender equality — calling for toilets that are safe, private and accessible for women, girls and everyone else, without discrimination.

Our context

Sanitation in urban Uttar Pradesh

Under the Swachh Bharat Mission-Urban, cities across Uttar Pradesh have built household, community and public toilets and moved towards open defecation free status. Under SBM-U 2.0, the focus has shifted to sustaining these gains — safe and well-maintained toilets, safe management of faecal sludge and used water, and the dignity and safety of sanitation workers.

Five-day State-wide sanitation campaign

On World Toilet Day, every urban local body in Uttar Pradesh runs a five-day campaign that puts community and public toilets at the centre, following the guidance of the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India. Toilets are checked against five FACES parameters.

Day-wise plan (suggestive)

  • Day 1
    Thu, 19 Nov
    Inauguration of new community, public and aspirational toilets; campaign launch on World Toilet Day
  • Day 2
    Fri, 20 Nov
    Public audit of CTs/PTs on the FACES parameters, led by women SHGs and community groups
  • Day 3
    Sat, 21 Nov
    Repair, refurbishment and upgrading; water, light, handwashing, menstrual hygiene and accessibility
  • Day 4
    Sun, 22 Nov
    Special cleanliness drives and awareness in schools, markets and RWAs; sustaining ODF status
  • Day 5
    Mon, 23 Nov
    Toilet locator and complaint signage, felicitation of sanitation workers, campaign close and State Conclave

ULBs may change the order to suit local needs, but all activities are to be held during the campaign.
